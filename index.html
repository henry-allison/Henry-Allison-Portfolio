About

Henry Allison is a film producer and editor based in Salt Lake City, Utah. His work spans documentary, commercial, and branded spheres.

He is the co-producer of Noah Kahan: Out of Body, a feature-length documentary about the musician Noah Kahan, now streaming on Netflix.

Henry is a partner at Brown Barn Studios, a documentary production company that specializes in helping businesses and organizations demonstrate their impact.

He also writes on Substack and enjoys skiing in the Wasatch mountains and challenging local seniors to a lively game of pickleball.
